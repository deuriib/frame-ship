# PRV — Privacy (Ley 172-13)

- **PRV-01** minimization; purpose limitation; collect only what is necessary.
- **PRV-02** every port, adapter, event, log, prompt, export, form, campaign, invoice, HR record is a PII checkpoint — mask/tokenize, allowlists only.
- **PRV-03** every PII store declares purpose + TTL + deletion procedure; automated enforcement.
- **PRV-04** PASS exports carry allowlisted evidence only; no raw PII.
- **PRV-05** no PII in test data — synthetic or anonymized only.
- **PRV-06** data subject rights — access, rectification, erasure, objection — within legal timeframe; procedure with SLA and evidence trail.
- **PRV-07** cross-border transfers only to approved jurisdictions with adequate protection and DPAs.
- **PRV-08** DPIA for high-risk processing; privacy by design and default; ROPA maintained; DPAs with all processors.
- **PRV-09** breach notification within 72h to authority and affected parties per law.
- **PRV-10** sensitive data (medical, biometric, special categories) requires explicit consent, strict need-to-know, legal review; never in general files or shared drives.
