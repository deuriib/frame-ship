# MKT — Marketing

- **MKT-01** no false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims; evidence on file per claim.
- **MKT-02** comparative advertising only with verified data and legal review.
- **MKT-03** testimonials real, consented, incentivization disclosed; no fabricated or edited reviews.
- **MKT-04** pricing, discounts, availability accurate and current; no bait-and-switch; no dark patterns.
- **MKT-05** influencer and affiliate disclosures clear, prominent, before the fold, per platform and law.
- **MKT-06** consent per law and platform rules; opt-in for email/SMS/WhatsApp; granular cookies; opt-out honored within legal time.
- **MKT-07** suppression lists maintained and enforced across all channels.
- **MKT-08** no PII in campaigns, creatives, UTM params, or ad audiences beyond allowlisted fields; segmentation only on consented, minimized data.
- **MKT-09** data subject rights honored across all marketing systems; deletion propagated to ad platforms and CRMs.
- **MKT-10** brand guidelines enforced (voice, tone, logo, colors); no off-brand assets.
- **MKT-11** accessibility: alt text, captions, transcripts, contrast, readable fonts; WCAG 2.1 AA where applicable.
- **MKT-12** no unlicensed assets; license on file per image, music, font, footage.
- **MKT-13** no AI-generated content published without human review, fact-check, and disclosure where required.
- **MKT-14** no culturally insensitive, discriminatory, or politically risky content without review.
- **MKT-15** platform policies respected; accounts use MFA and least privilege; no purchased followers, engagement, or fake reviews; no spam; no ToS-violating scraping.
- **MKT-16** social crisis protocol: who speaks, what is approved, escalation path, legal review for sensitive topics.
- **MKT-17** scheduled content reviewed before publish; no auto-publish of unreviewed drafts.
- **MKT-18** metrics defined consistently (CAC, LTV, ROAS, conversion); no vanity metrics as business results; attribution methodology documented; no double-counting or hidden spend.
- **MKT-19** A/B tests statistically valid, hypothesis pre-registered, no dark patterns, no harm; budget tracked per channel; no overspend without approval.
