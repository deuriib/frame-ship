# LEG — Legal

- **LEG-01** every contract reviewed by legal before signature; no verbal commitments; no side letters.
- **LEG-02** obligation register maintained: deliverables, SLAs, penalties, termination, renewal dates, owners.
- **LEG-03** standard templates only; deviations require legal approval and a recorded decision.
- **LEG-04** no auto-renewal without review 60 days before expiry; no unilateral changes accepted.
- **LEG-05** IP ownership, licensing, assignment verified; open-source usage cleared via license scan.
- **LEG-06** compliance register: Ley 172-13, labor, tax, sector-specific, consumer protection; every processing mapped to legal basis.
- **LEG-07** no legal advice from non-lawyers; route to legal.
- **LEG-08** liability, indemnity, warranty clauses reviewed per contract; insurance coverage verified for new activities (cyber, E&O, D&O).
- **LEG-09** litigation hold on notice; no deletion of potentially relevant records; conflicts of interest disclosed and managed.
- **LEG-10** retention schedule enforced with automated deletion after TTL; immutable audit trail; all legal documents versioned.
