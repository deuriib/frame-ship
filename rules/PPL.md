# PPL — CHRO / CPO (People)

- **PPL-01** roles approved by budget owner + HR before posting; no phantom reqs; job descriptions reviewed for bias, clarity, legal compliance.
- **PPL-02** structured interviews with defined competencies and scorecards; interviewers trained; no unstructured gut-feel decisions.
- **PPL-03** no discriminatory criteria (age, gender, origin, religion, disability, pregnancy, union); reasonable accommodations provided.
- **PPL-04** background checks and references lawful, role-relevant, consented; adverse action documented.
- **PPL-05** offers approved by HR + Finance + hiring manager; no verbal offers; no off-band compensation without written approval.
- **PPL-06** onboarding checklist enforced — contract, ID, tax, benefits, equipment, access, training; access per least privilege; day-one offboarding revocation.
- **PPL-07** employee PII is a PII store under PRV-03: purpose, TTL, deletion declared and enforced.
- **PPL-08** medical, biometric, and sensitive data: explicit consent, strict need-to-know, legal review; never in general files or shared drives.
- **PPL-09** employee data access role-based, audited, reviewed quarterly; no bulk exports without approval.
- **PPL-10** employee data subject rights honored within legal timeframe; cross-border transfers only to approved jurisdictions with DPAs.
- **PPL-11** goals documented and reviewed on cadence; no surprise terminations without documented performance trail.
- **PPL-12** PIPs written, time-bound, supported; never a pretext for discrimination.
- **PPL-13** promotion and compensation decisions on documented criteria; calibrated across teams; no favoritism.
- **PPL-14** mandatory training: security awareness, privacy (Ley 172-13), anti-harassment, code of conduct, role-specific compliance.
- **PPL-15** pay bands defined, benchmarked, reviewed annually; no off-band pay without written approval; pay equity reviewed and remediated.
- **PPL-16** benefits administered per policy and law; no undocumented promises.
- **PPL-17** payroll dual approval, reconciliation, on-time filings; no off-cycle payments without approval.
- **PPL-18** code of conduct signed by all; violations investigated promptly, fairly, documented; no retaliation.
- **PPL-19** anti-harassment and anti-discrimination enforced; confidential and protected reporting channels.
- **PPL-20** whistleblower channel available and protected.
- **PPL-21** workplace safety per law; incidents reported, investigated, remediated.
- **PPL-22** remote/hybrid policies documented; equipment, security, and privacy requirements enforced.
- **PPL-23** disciplinary actions documented, consistent, legally reviewed before execution; HR + Legal sign-off where required.
- **PPL-24** offboarding: access revoked same day, assets returned, final pay per law, exit interview offered, knowledge transfer documented.
- **PPL-25** no deletion of records under litigation hold or retention schedule.
- **PPL-26** redundancy and restructuring per law: consultation, notice, severance, documentation.
- **PPL-27** HR metrics tracked — headcount, voluntary/involuntary attrition, time-to-hire, engagement, diversity, pay equity, compliance training completion, ER cases; no vanity metrics.
- **PPL-28** HR policies versioned, published, acknowledged annually.
