# REV — CRO / Revenue

- **REV-01** CRM is the single source of truth; no shadow pipelines; deals not in CRM do not exist for forecast.
- **REV-02** every deal has owner, stage, amount, close date, next step, buyer-intent evidence; stage definitions documented and enforced.
- **REV-03** no stage advancement without exit criteria; no sandbagging or happy-ears; forecast submitted on schedule with confidence and assumptions.
- **REV-04** weighted pipeline methodology documented and consistently applied; no manual reweighting to hit targets.
- **REV-05** discounts, non-standard terms, and deviations require approval per threshold matrix; no verbal commitments; no side agreements.
- **REV-06** contract terms, SLAs, and pricing reviewed by legal and finance before signature; no signature by unauthorized roles.
- **REV-07** no revenue recognition before delivery/acceptance; no pull-forward or push-out without documented justification and finance approval.
- **REV-08** every closed-won/lost deal has a reason code; material deals get a post-mortem; no vanity metrics.
- **REV-09** pricing changes approved by pricing committee (CRO + Finance + Product); no ad-hoc pricing per rep.
- **REV-10** discount floors enforced in CPQ; overrides logged, time-bound, reviewed monthly.
- **REV-11** packaging and entitlements accurate in billing; no unbilled usage or overbilling; monthly CRM/CPQ/billing reconciliation.
- **REV-12** onboarding milestones with owners and SLAs; no silent churn.
- **REV-13** health scores defined, monitored, acted on; documented escalation for at-risk accounts.
- **REV-14** renewals and expansions forecast separately from new business; auto-renewals reviewed 90 days before expiry.
- **REV-15** churn reasons coded and analyzed, fed back to product and marketing; no hidden churn by reclassification.
- **REV-16** no customer PII in sales tools beyond allowlisted fields; masked per PRV-02.
- **REV-17** no bribes, kickbacks, or improper gifts to buyers; gifts register per FIN-12.
- **REV-18** no misrepresentation of capabilities, roadmap, or security posture; only approved collateral.
- **REV-19** no pressure tactics violating consumer protection or platform rules; no dark patterns in trials or cancellations.
- **REV-20** conflicts of interest disclosed (buyer relationships, side businesses).
- **REV-21** commission plans documented, signed, versioned; no retroactive changes without written approval.
- **REV-22** SPIFFs and contests approved by finance and HR; no off-plan payments; clawbacks per policy, consistently enforced.
