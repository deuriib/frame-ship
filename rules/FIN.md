# FIN — Finance

- **FIN-01** segregation of duties: no single person initiates, approves, and reconciles; enforced in systems.
- **FIN-02** documented approval thresholds for spend, payment, refund, discount, write-off; dual approval above threshold.
- **FIN-03** no manual journal entries without support and secondary review; no off-books cash; no unrecorded liabilities; no spreadsheets as source of truth.
- **FIN-04** bank account changes require out-of-band call-back and dual approval.
- **FIN-05** budget owner per cost center; no spend without budget line and owner approval; POs above threshold; no verbal POs; no splitting to avoid thresholds.
- **FIN-06** vendor onboarding requires legal, security, finance review; no duplicate or ghost vendors.
- **FIN-07** receipts required; no personal expenses on company accounts; no undocumented reimbursements.
- **FIN-08** subscription and SaaS inventory maintained; renewals reviewed; no orphaned subscriptions.
- **FIN-09** double-entry accrual; monthly reconciliations; enforced close checklist; sign-off on close; audit trail for every adjustment.
- **FIN-10** revenue recognition per standards; no premature or deferred recognition without policy; FX, intercompany, transfer pricing documented.
- **FIN-11** financial statements reviewed by CFO/controller before external release.
- **FIN-12** no bribes, kickbacks, facilitation payments, or gifts beyond policy; gifts register maintained.
- **FIN-13** sanctions and PEP screening for vendors, customers, partners; whistleblower channel available and protected; no retaliation.
- **FIN-14** anomaly monitoring: duplicate payments, round amounts, unusual vendors, off-hours entries.
- **FIN-15** cash flow forecast maintained; liquidity buffer per policy; no unauthorized borrowing or hedging; no speculative trading.
- **FIN-16** customer credit limits enforced; dunning documented; bad debt provisioning per policy.
- **FIN-17** tax filings on time; no informal arrangements; transfer pricing documentation maintained; VAT/sales tax per jurisdiction; nexus monitored.
