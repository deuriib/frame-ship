# Growing Talent

## Overview

**Growth IS potential evidence turned into a plan, reviewed until it's a promotion case or a pivot.**

You identify potential with evidence (not tenure, not confidence), write plans with one growth bet each, and review quarterly. No promotion without sustained next-level proof.

**Core principle:** If the development plan has five goals, it has zero. One bet, one behavior, one quarter.

## The Iron Law

```
NO PROMOTION OR SUCCESSION SLATE WITHOUT SUSTAINED NEXT-LEVEL EVIDENCE
```

If the person hasn't demonstrated next-level behaviors over time (not one heroic month), they are not ready — develop, don't anoint.

## Checklist

Create a todo per item. Complete in order.

1. **Potential signal** — performance (delivers current level) + aspiration (wants it) + ability (learns fast, handles ambiguity). All three with examples. Two of three = develop the missing one, don't promote.
2. **Career path** — level rubric for the track (IC and manager), what changes per level, lateral options. No rubric → no fair growth.
3. **Development plan** — ONE growth bet per quarter: behavior to build, reps to get it, support named, measure of done. Signed by person + manager.
4. **Reps** — stretch assignment, mentoring, training, or rotation matched to the bet. Training without reps is entertainment.
5. **Quarterly review** — evidence against the bet: kept, extended, or pivoted. Log it.
6. **Succession slate** — per critical role: ready-now (evidence), ready-1yr (gap + plan), emergency cover. Reviewed twice a year, not in a crisis.
7. **Promotion case** — sustained next-level evidence + business need + calibration sign-off. Then communicate with the evidence story.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

MUST:
1. Pay bands benchmarked and reviewed annually. Documented criteria + calibration behind every promotion and comp change.
2. Mandatory training completed and logged: security, privacy (Ley 172-13), anti-harassment, conduct, role compliance.
3. Policies versioned, published, acknowledged. Reviewed annually.
4. Pay equity reviewed. Disparities remediated with timeline. Retaliation for raising equity concerns banned.
5. Payroll dual-approved + reconciled. Tax on time. No off-cycle payments without written approval.
6. Benefits per policy and law only. No undocumented promises in growth conversations.

NEVER:
1. Favoritism hires, promotions, or HiPo picks without published criteria.
2. Off-band moves without written justification.
3. Secret HiPo lists or unpublished promotion bars.
4. Sensitive data (medical, biometric, beliefs) in development plans or succession slates.
5. Promotion to retain without sustained next-level evidence.

ESCALATE immediately to CHRO/CPO + Legal + protect confidentiality:
- Discrimination, harassment, or retaliation signals in growth decisions.
- Legal claims, DSR requests, or cross-border data moves tied to a person.
- No silent remediation. Log the escalation, freeze affected decisions.

## 9-Box Done Right

- 9-box sorts conversations, never people. It's a placement of evidence, not a label on a forehead.
- Never share raw 9-box placement with the person. Share the behaviors and the plan.
- Low-output/high-potential ("rough diamond") gets a 90-day bet or an honest exit conversation — not two years of hope.
- High-output/low-potential ("trusted expert") is a success, not a failure. Grow depth, don't force breadth.

## Mentoring & HiPo Rules

- Mentor writes the plan WITH the mentee, meets monthly minimum, ends with a retro. Open-ended mentoring drifts into coffee chats.
- HiPo programs: selection criteria published, cohort diverse by construction, exit honest when the bet fails. Secret HiPo lists breed cynicism.
- Every stretch assignment has a safety net named (sponsor, rollback, support). Stretch without safety is setup-to-fail.

## Red Flags — STOP

| Thought | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| "They're ready, just promote them" | Ready means sustained next-level evidence + need. Show both. |
| "High potential, fast-track everything" | Fast-tracking without reps creates leaders people quit under. Reps first. |
| "Development plan with five goals" | Five goals = zero focus. One bet per quarter. |
| "They don't want feedback, they're senior" | Seniority raises the feedback bar, never lowers it. |
| "Succession can wait until next year" | Succession decided in a crisis is panic with titles. Slate now. |
| "Training budget spent = growth done" | Seats in courses ≠ behaviors changed. Measure the bet, not the attendance. |
| "They're happy, no plan needed" | Content today ≠ growing tomorrow. Everyone gets a bet, including top performers. |

## Verification

Before approving ANY promotion, succession slate, or HiPo selection:

1. **IDENTIFY:** Level rubric + evidence log + calibration note — all three exist?
2. **RUN:** Read the evidence against next-level behaviors, sustained over time.
3. **READ:** Business need real? Bar consistent with peers? Gaps named with plans?
4. **VERIFY:** Gaps → develop, don't promote. Full coverage → promote WITH the evidence story.
5. **ONLY THEN:** Communicate, then run people:verifying-people-decisions on the written announcement.

## When To Apply

- Career paths, level rubrics, IC/manager tracks
- Development plans and growth bets
- Mentoring programs, HiPo cohorts
- Succession planning and emergency cover
- Promotions, lateral moves, rotations
- Training strategy tied to growth bets
