# Reviewing Performance

## Overview

**Performance review IS evidence-first calibration applied to people growth.**

You gather evidence continuously, calibrate across peers before communicating, and only then deliver. No rating, raise, PIP, or promotion without a calibrated evidence record.

**Core principle:** If the person is surprised in the review, the process already failed — feedback should never debut in a rating meeting.

## The Iron Law

```
NO RATING, RAISE, PIP, OR PROMOTION WITHOUT CALIBRATED EVIDENCE
```

If the evidence log doesn't exist and peers haven't calibrated the bar, you cannot communicate the decision.

## Checklist

Create a todo per item. Complete in order.

1. **Evidence log** — outcomes vs. agreed goals, 2-3 strength examples with impact, 1-2 growth examples with impact, peer/customer quotes. No adjectives without receipts.
2. **Self-review** — person writes their own evidence first. Compare gaps before you judge.
3. **Draft rating** — against the level rubric, not against teammates. Name the level behaviors demonstrated or missing.
4. **Calibration** — with peer managers: same bar, blind to names first where possible. Adjust the draft, log why it moved.
5. **Decision package** — rating + compensation impact + growth plan in one page. Get human-partner approval BEFORE communicating.
6. **Deliver** — in person (or video), evidence first, rating second, growth third. End with one agreed next action and a check-in date.
7. **PIP (only if needed)** — 30/60/90 with measurable bar, weekly check-ins, support named, consequence explicit. A PIP without support is paperwork for an exit — don't write those.

## Calibration Rules

- Calibrate the BAR, not the person. "Would this evidence earn this rating on any team?" — if not, the rating moves, not the person.
- Ratings distribution is a diagnostic, not a quota. Forced curves without evidence are politics.
- Promotion = sustained next-level evidence + business need for the level. One great quarter is not a promotion case.
- Compensation changes reference the band and the evidence. "Market adjustment" without band data is a guess — get the data.
- Document the calibration delta: draft rating → final rating → reason. Memory lies; the log doesn't.

## Feedback Rules

- SBI always: Situation → Behavior → Impact. "You did great" is not feedback.
- One growth theme per cycle, not five. Five themes = zero change.
- Feedback within days, not quarters. The review summarizes; it never debuts.
- 1:1s are the delivery system: weekly, person-owned agenda, growth item always present.

## Red Flags — STOP

| Thought | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| "They know what this is about" | If it wasn't written and discussed, they don't. No ambush reviews. |
| "Below expectations, let's PIP immediately" | A PIP without prior documented feedback is a legal and trust failure. Evidence first. |
| "Top performer, skip the process" | Top performers deserve the clearest evidence — it's their promotion case. |
| "Calibration is bureaucracy" | Uncalibrated ratings are favoritism with a form. Calibrate. |
| "Let's not write it down for now" | Unwritten feedback didn't happen. Write it. |
| "Promotion to retain them" | Retention promotions inflate levels and deflate trust. Promote on evidence + need. |
| "Comp stays quiet, easier that way" | Opaque comp breeds distrust. Share band, position, and reasoning. |

## Rationalization Prevention

| Excuse | Reality |
|--------|---------|
| "Should be obvious from the results" | Results without behaviors mislead. Show both. |
| "I'm confident in the rating" | Confidence ≠ calibration. Calibrate first. |
| "Just this once, skip calibration" | No exceptions on ratings that move pay or level. |
| "The team agrees" | Agreement without independent evidence is groupthink. Log evidence per manager. |
| "Different words so the process doesn't apply" | Spirit over letter. Evidence before communication, always. |

## Verification

Before communicating ANY rating, raise, PIP, or promotion:

1. **IDENTIFY:** What evidence proves this decision? (log + rubric + calibration note)
2. **RUN:** Read the full evidence log and the calibration delta.
3. **READ:** Every claim has a receipt? Bar applied consistently?
4. **VERIFY:** Gaps → collect or downgrade the claim. Full coverage → communicate WITH the evidence.
5. **ONLY THEN:** Deliver, then invoke people:verifying-people-decisions if the message leaves your hands in writing.

## When To Apply

- Annual / mid-year / quarterly reviews
- Continuous feedback and 1:1 systems
- Calibration sessions
- PIPs and performance exits
- Merit raises, bonuses, promotions, level changes
- Founder / executive feedback to managers
