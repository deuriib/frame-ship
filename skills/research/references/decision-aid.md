# Research Decision Aid

## When to Declare Research (YES)

- **Novel Domain**: The team has no prior experience with the technology or business sector.
- **High Uncertainty**: The desired outcome is clear, but the 'how' involves significant unknowns.
- **Data Dependency**: The initiative depends on external data, APIs, or legal constraints not yet documented.
- **Ambiguous Requirements**: The initial request is too vague to form a measurable OKR.

## When to Waive Research (NO/WAIVED)

- **Familiar Domain**: The team has successfully shipped similar features recently.
- **Settled Requirements**: The request comes with a detailed set of pre-validated requirements.
- **Trivial Scope**: The change is a small iteration on an existing flow.
- **Spike-Equivalent**: The goal is a quick answer, not a full initiative (use the spike path).

## Verdict Thresholds (named, owned — not prose)

| Term | Definition | Checked at |
|------|-----------|------------|
| **Evidence is sufficient** | ≥2 independent sources in ≥2 different channels, at least one quantitative (n ≥ 30 records or ≥5 interviews), each openable by a second person, no open contradiction | step 3 |
| **Contradicted** | ≥2 independent sources disagree — the claim that depended on it is killed | step 3 |
| **GO** | Every blocking question answered with sufficient evidence; no contradicted claim remains | step 5 |
| **NO-GO** | A blocking question is unanswered, or a contradicted claim undermines the premise | step 5 |
| **PIVOT** | The evidence answers the question but refutes the assumed direction — re-scope, don't proceed | step 5 |

## Decision Authority

The verdict is issued by the research specialist and **owned by a named human** when it commits budget or scope. NO-GO is a normal, non-punished outcome; silence is not approval.
