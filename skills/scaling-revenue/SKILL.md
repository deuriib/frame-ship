# Scaling Revenue — Compound What Already Works

Turn signed clients into upsells, renewals, and referrals — measured on one scorecard, grown one lever at a time.

New acquisition is the most expensive revenue. This skill harvests the cheapest first.

## Prerequisites

At least one signed client from closing-deals. If none exists, invoke the earlier flywheel skills first. Scaling zero clients is multiplying by zero.

## The Four Levers

Work one lever per month. Finish it, measure it, then move on:

1. **Upsell — more value per client.** Map the next problem your delivery reveals. Offer it as the Premium tier move or a second engagement, priced on its own ROI. Rule: never upsell before the first outcome lands. Ask at the win, not during the struggle.
2. **Retention — keep the revenue.** Name the renewal moment upfront (date + metric). Send a one-line win report before every invoice so paying feels like winning. Churn signal: silence. Silence gets a call, not an email.
3. **Referrals — borrow trust.** Ask at the win ("who else has this pain?"), not at the end. Script: "Who's one person you'd feel good introducing me to — I'd treat them like I treated you?" One name beats "let me know if you hear of anyone."
4. **Metrics — see the machine.** One scorecard, reviewed monthly (30 min): pipeline (touches → replies → calls), sales (calls → closes → cash collected), delivery (outcomes hit, days to outcome), compounding (upsell %, renewal %, referrals per client). Fix the worst number first.

## Monthly Review

30 minutes, same day each month:

1. Read the scorecard out loud. Worst number gets named.
2. Trace it to its skill: bad replies → outbound-prospecting, bad closes → closing-deals, bad renewals → delivery or retention lever.
3. One fix, one owner, one date. Everything else waits.

## Anti-Patterns

- **New channel before referrals.** Referrals close 3-5× better than cold. Ask every win before opening new acquisition.
- **Upselling strugglers.** Selling more to an unhappy client accelerates churn and bad reviews. Win first, expand second.
- **Vanity metrics.** Followers and impressions don't invoice. Track touches, closes, cash, renewals, referrals.
- **Fixing everything monthly.** Four levers, one per cycle. Parallel fixes teach nothing about what worked.

## Done Looks Like

A monthly scorecard habit with one lever improving per cycle: upsell attach rate rising, renewals on calendar, one referral ask per win. When a lever needs its own craft, return to its skill: outbound-prospecting for pipeline, closing-deals for win rate, pricing-packaging for raises.
