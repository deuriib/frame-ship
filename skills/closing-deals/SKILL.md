# Closing Deals — Run the Call, Win the Yes

Turn booked calls into signed clients through discovery first, diagnosis second, prescription last — never pitch before pain.

No proposal goes out without a discovery call. No discount without a trade.

## Prerequisites

An approved designing-offer sheet and pricing-offers card. If either is missing, invoke those skills first. Quoting without tiers turns every call into custom haggling.

## The Call Flow

Follow this sequence on every sales call:

1. **Set the agenda (2 min).** "Here's how I'd like to use our time — your situation, what's costing you, whether I can help, and next steps either way. Fair?" Permission first, control always.
2. **Discovery (60% of the call).** Pain, cost, past attempts, decision process. Ask until you can price their pain in their words: "So this costs you roughly $X/month — did I get that right?" If you can't price it, you can't close it.
3. **Diagnosis.** Play back their situation tighter than they said it. "Here's what I'm hearing..." Buyers sign with whoever understands them best.
4. **Prescription.** Present the ONE tier that fits, anchored to their ROI math from step 2. Never present all three tiers on the call — recommend, don't menu.
5. **Ask (one sentence).** "Want to do this?" Then silence. Whoever speaks first loses. Objections are questions — answer, then ask again.

## Objection Playbook

| They say | You do |
|----------|--------|
| "Too expensive" | Return to their ROI math: "Against the $X/month this costs you, Core pays back in N weeks. Which part of the math feels off?" |
| "Need to think" | "Of course. What's the one thing you'd need to be sure of to say yes?" Isolate it, answer it, ask again. |
| "Send a proposal" | "Happy to. So it's useful, what's the decision process after you read it?" No process = no proposal. Proposal without a reader is a trash-can document. |
| "Not now" | "When should we talk — and what changes by then?" Calendar it or kill it. "Someday" is a no with extra steps. |
| "Talk to my partner" | "Let's bring them in now or book all three of us. I don't want you reselling my offer for me." |

## Guardrails

- **Nothing verbal outside the proposal.** If it isn't on the one-pager, it wasn't promised. Side agreements and call-only commitments don't exist.
- **No stage advance without exit criteria.** A deal moves to proposal only after discovery priced their pain; moves to signed only with upfront collected and a start date. "Feels close" is not a stage.
- **Won/lost gets a reason code.** Every closed file logs why (price, timing, fit, ghosted, lost-to-X). No reason = the file stays open and counts against you.
- **Conflicts and gifts disclosed.** Any referral fee, finder payment, or gift over token value goes in writing before the proposal. Undisclosed money on a deal kills trust permanently.

## Proposal Rules

One page: their pain in their words, the recommended tier, ROI math, guarantee, start date, payment terms. Expires in 7 days. Follow up twice, then close the file. Dead proposals get a breakup line that invites referrals.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Pitching first.** Solution before diagnosis reads as generic. Discovery earns the right to prescribe.
- **Discounting without trade.** Every concession needs scope, speed, or terms coming back. Free concessions train hagglers.
- **Chasing maybes.** A fast no funds the next yes. Pipeline full of maybes is pipeline theater.
- **Skipping the ask.** "Let me know" is not a close. Ask the sentence, hold the silence.

## Done Looks Like

Signed client on a defined tier with upfront collected and a start date — or a clean no with a referral ask logged. Signed clients go to scaling-revenue. Next skill: scaling-revenue.
