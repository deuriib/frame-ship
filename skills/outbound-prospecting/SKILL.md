# Outbound Prospecting — Open Conversations That Book Calls

Turn an approved offer and pricing into touches that earn replies and booked calls — measured weekly, killed fast when dead.

No booked call happens without touches. No touches without this skill's sequence.

## Prerequisites

An approved designing-offer sheet and pricing-packaging card. If either is missing, invoke those skills first. Outbound for a vague promise burns the list and your reputation.

## The Sequence

Produce this artifact and get approval, then execute one channel at a time:

1. **List (50 names).** Named people matching the opportunity brief's buyer, with one personalization hook each (post, hire, launch, problem signal). No list = no outbound. Quality beats volume: 50 right people beat 500 suspects.
2. **Opener (under 50 words).** Hook (their signal) + pain (their words) + one low-friction question. No pitch, no links, no "I'd love to". One message, one question.
3. **Follow-ups (3 touches).** New angle each time, not "bumping this up": bump with proof, bump with teardown, breakup that invites a no. Space 3-4 days apart. Stop at "no".
4. **Call booking.** One link, two time options, zero friction. Confirm with agenda in one line so show-up rates hold.
5. **Weekly scorecard.** Sent / replies / positive replies / calls booked / show rate. Review every Friday, 15 minutes: keep, fix, or kill the sequence.

## Kill Criteria

After 100 touches on one sequence: <5% reply rate = rewrite the opener. <10% of replies positive = the pain is wrong, return to discovering-opportunities. Show rate <50% = fix the confirmation, not the opener.

## Guardrails

- **Personalization must be verifiable.** Every hook cites something real (their post, hire, launch). Fabricated familiarity burns the domain and the name.
- **No means no, opt-out means out.** "No", "not interested", or opt-out ends the sequence immediately. Arguing or re-adding them poisons the channel for every future campaign.
- **One channel until 100 touches.** No second channel before the first hits 100 touches and a Friday review. Parallel sequences teach nothing.
- **No pressure tactics or dark patterns.** No fake scarcity ("only 1 spot" when there are 10), no fake familiarity ("great chatting yesterday" when it never happened), nothing the platform would ban.
- **PII minimum.** Collect only what the sequence needs (name, role, public signal). No scraping private data, no buying shady lists.

## Questions That Matter

Ask at most 3, one round, then draft:

1. Which single channel this week — email, LinkedIn, DMs, or one community?
2. Where do 50 named buyers come from — existing list, search, referrals?
3. What proof or teardown can you give away free in touch two?

## Anti-Patterns

- **Pitch-slapping.** Feature lists in touch one get ignored and reported. Earn the reply first.
- **Multichannel chaos.** Three half-run channels teach nothing. One channel, 100 touches, then judge.
- **Follow-up shame.** 60%+ of replies come after touch two. No follow-up = leaving money unsent.
- **Arguing with no.** A clean no beats a slow maybe. Thank, ask for the referral, move on.

## Done Looks Like

An approved sequence (list source, opener, 3 follow-ups, booking flow) plus a weekly scorecard habit. Calls booked go to closing-deals. Next skill: closing-deals.
