# Shaping Culture

## Overview

**Culture IS the behavior the org rewards, repeated until it's identity.**

You measure what people experience, fix rituals and incentives before structure, and only reorg when the work demands it. No values poster without a ritual that proves it.

**Core principle:** If the value doesn't change a decision, a calendar invite, or a promotion — it's decoration.

## The Iron Law

```
NO REORG WITHOUT DIAGNOSIS EVIDENCE
```

If you can't name the broken workflow with data (not anecdotes), you cannot move boxes.

## Order of Operations

Reorg is LAST, not first:

1. **Measure** — engagement/climate data, exit themes, decision latency, meeting load. Numbers before narratives.
2. **Rituals** — fix the recurring behaviors (1:1s, retros, planning, recognition) that produce the culture daily.
3. **Incentives** — align what gets promoted, bonused, and praised with the values claimed.
4. **Structure** — only when steps 1-3 prove the boxes are the bottleneck: spans, layers, ownership, decision rights.

## Checklist

Create a todo per item. Complete in order.

1. **Baseline** — current engagement data, turnover by segment, exit themes, eNPS or equivalent. No baseline → no before/after claim later.
2. **Values → behaviors** — each value gets 2-3 observable behaviors + 1 anti-behavior. "Ownership" means nothing until it means "writes the follow-up before leaving the room".
3. **Rituals** — onboarding program, 1:1 cadence, retros, all-hands, recognition. Owner + cadence + artifact per ritual.
4. **Onboarding program** — pre-boarding checklist, week-1 outcomes, 30-60-90 per role family, buddy assigned, manager check-ins scheduled.
5. **Org design (if needed)** — design principles, spans/layers target, decision rights (DACI per key decision), transition plan with dates and owners.
6. **Offboarding** — exit interview, knowledge transfer checklist, alumni treatment, access revocation. Exits are culture broadcasts — handle them with excellence.
7. **Measure again** — 90 days later, same instrument. Report delta honestly, including what didn't move.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

MUST:
1. Code of conduct signed by all. Violations investigated promptly, fairly, documented.
2. Confidential reporting channels published + monitored. Whistleblower protection enforced.
3. Anti-harassment / anti-discrimination enforced in every ritual, hire, review, and promotion.
4. Remote/hybrid policy documented: equipment, security, privacy enforced.
5. Workplace safety per law. Incidents remediated with owner + date.
6. Policies versioned, published, acknowledged. Reviewed annually.

NEVER:
1. Retaliation against reporters. Ever. Monitor after every investigation.
2. Silent remediation of harassment, discrimination, or safety issues.
3. Reorg or restructure without legal path: consultation, notice, severance, docs.
4. Deletion of records under litigation hold or retention schedule.
5. Exits without same-day access revocation, asset return, final pay per law, exit interview + knowledge transfer.

ESCALATE immediately to CHRO/CPO + Legal + protect confidentiality:
- Harassment, discrimination, safety, breach, or legal claim signals.
- Litigation hold or regulator contact. Freeze deletions, preserve evidence.
- Log the escalation, restrict to need-to-know.

## Org Design Rules

- Design from decisions backward: who decides what, with what input, by when. Boxes follow decisions, not the reverse.
- Spans: 5-8 for managers doing real management. Layers: fewest that preserve coaching quality.
- One owner per outcome. Shared ownership is unowned work.
- Reorg communication: reason → criteria → new shape → transition → support. In that order, all in writing.
- Never reorg to solve a person problem. Coach, move, or exit the person — don't redraw the org around them.

## Ritual Rules

- Every ritual has an owner, a cadence, and an artifact. Ownerless rituals die in three weeks.
- Kill one ritual for every ritual added. Calendar debt is culture debt.
- Onboarding is a program, not a day. 90-day outcomes or it didn't happen.
- Offboarding with dignity: grateful, clear, fast on logistics, generous on words. How people leave teaches everyone watching.

## Red Flags — STOP

| Thought | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| "Let's reorg, that'll fix it" | Reorgs fix structure problems. Most culture problems are ritual problems. Diagnose first. |
| "New values will align everyone" | Values without behaviors and incentives are wallpaper. Behaviors first. |
| "Engagement survey says we're fine" | One instrument never says you're fine. Triangulate: survey + exits + turnover + 1:1 themes. |
| "Onboarding is HR's job" | Onboarding is the hiring manager's job with HR's system. Unowned onboarding = 90-day turnover. |
| "Let's not over-communicate the change" | Under-communication is the #1 reorg killer. Written, repeated, two-way. |
| "Exit interviews don't change anything" | Then you're asking wrong. Theme them quarterly and act on top-2. |
| "Culture is soft stuff for later" | Culture is what ships while you focus on "hard stuff". Design it now. |

## Verification

Before announcing ANY culture, ritual, onboarding, reorg, or offboarding change:

1. **IDENTIFY:** Baseline data + design + communication draft — all three exist?
2. **RUN:** Read the baseline; read the ritual/structure doc; read the comms out loud.
3. **READ:** Behaviors observable? Owners named? Dates real? Support explicit?
4. **VERIFY:** Gaps → fix before announcing. Full coverage → announce WITH the why and the measure-again date.
5. **ONLY THEN:** Communicate, then run people:verifying-people-decisions on the written announcement.

## When To Apply

- Engagement, climate, eNPS programs
- Values definition or refresh
- Ritual design (1:1s, retros, all-hands, recognition)
- Onboarding and offboarding programs
- Org design, spans/layers, decision rights, reorgs
- Mergers, new sites, remote/hybrid policy
